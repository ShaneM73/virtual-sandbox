Virtual AR Sandbox

Shape sand and watch water flow over it in real time. Both versions run the water solver from the UC Davis Augmented Reality Sandbox (SARndbox 2.8), ported to WebGL2, on a 160 × 120 cm virtual box.

Desktop

Mouse or touch. 3D and projector views, seven landscapes, terrain-model matching, infiltration, and ESRI ASCII grid export.

Open the desktop sandbox →

VR (Meta Quest)

Open in the Meta Quest Browser. The sandbox sits on a table in front of you. Sculpt with the right controller, make rain with the left, or switch to giant scale and walk through the landscape.

Open the VR sandbox →

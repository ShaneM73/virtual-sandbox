Looking for the bridge…

  1. Mount the Kinect about 1 m above the sand, looking straight down, with the whole box in view.
  2. Install Kinect for Windows SDK 1.8 from Microsoft, plug in the Kinect's power adapter and USB, then run SandboxBridge.exe.
  3. The camera view on the left should show the sandbox. Near things are bright, far things are dark.

Set up the box

  1. Flatten the sand to the level you want as sea level, then capture the base plane.
  2. Click the four inside corners of the box on the camera view, in this order: front-left, front-right, back-left, back-right. "Front" is the side where people stand.

No base plane yet.

Capture the base plane first.

Anything higher than the highest sand, like a hand, is ignored for the terrain and turns into rain instead.

Calibrate the projector

  1. Click Open projector window, move it to the projector, and click Go full screen there.
  2. Make a target: a CD or a 12 cm paper disk taped to the end of a thin stick.
  3. The projector shows a crosshair. Hold the disk flat so the crosshair lands on its center. Keep still; it captures automatically. Do each spot low (just above the sand), then the second round high (about 25 cm up).

AR Sandbox VR

Put on a Meta Quest and the sandbox sits on a table in front of you. Point with the right controller to dig and pile sand, and hold the left trigger to make it rain.

Checking for WebXR…

In the headset, the wrist panel shows the frame rate. Quest 2 runs at 72 fps; if it drops below about 68, pick a lower level.
